Effective as of 2026-08-23

This privacy policy applies to the よりみち app for mobile devices (the "Application"), operated by Taishu Wakasugi ("we", "us", or the "Service Provider").

Summary: The Application does not have a server or backend of its own. Location data you save is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to us. The only external party involved is Apple, which briefly processes your coordinates to look up an address (see "Apple Location Services" below).

Information We Collect

The Application collects only the location information described below. It does not collect your device's IP address, does not track which screens you visit or how long you use the app, and does not use analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or cookies of any kind.

Location Information

When you tap the "+" button on the map screen, the Application reads your device's current GPS coordinates, converts them into a readable address, and matches them against a list of Japanese municipalities that is bundled inside the app (no network request is made for this matching). This information — the address, coordinates, the identified prefecture/municipality, and the date/time saved — is stored using Apple's on-device SwiftData framework, inside the Application's private storage area on your device.

  • This data is used only to display your saved places and related municipality information within the Application.
  • This data is never sent to the Service Provider or to any analytics or advertising service. We have no server that receives, stores, or has access to it.

Apple Location Services (MapKit)

To convert your GPS coordinates into a readable address, the Application uses Apple's MapKit reverse geocoding service. This means your coordinates are sent to Apple at the moment you save a location, so that Apple can return an address. This exchange is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, not by the Service Provider. No other third party receives your location data.

Your Rights and How to Delete Your Data

Because your saved locations exist only on your device, you are always in full control of them:

  • Delete a single entry: On the "保存した市区町村" (Saved Municipalities) screen, use the Edit control to remove the entry you no longer want.
  • Delete everything at once: Uninstalling the Application permanently deletes all saved locations, since nothing is kept anywhere else.

Since the Service Provider never receives or stores this data, there is nothing on our end to access, correct, or delete on your behalf. If you have any questions about this, contact us at taishu_wakasugi@outlook.com.

Your California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

The Service Provider does not sell or share personal information, and does not collect personal information on any server. If you are a California resident and have questions about this, contact us at taishu_wakasugi@outlook.com.

Children

Security

Your saved locations are protected by the security and sandboxing built into iOS itself, since they never leave your device. The Service Provider does not operate any server or database that could be affected by a data breach, because we do not receive or store your data.
